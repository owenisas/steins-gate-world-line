## 0. Most design-relevant facts (at a glance)

- Setting: Akihabara, Tokyo, summer 2010. The story begins **July 28, 2010** at the Radio Kaikan
  building. [WP-SG]
- The signature object is the **Divergence Meter**: 8 Nixie tubes showing a 7-digit value in the form
  `D.DDDDDD`. Examples: `0.571024` in episode 1, `1.048596` at the end. [F-DM][DENGEKI-DM][TARI]
- In the story's cosmology, the integer digit names the attractor field: `0.xxxxxx` = Alpha (Mayuri
  dies, SERN dystopia), `1.xxxxxx` = Beta (Kurisu dies, WWIII). **1.048596** = Steins Gate, which
  belongs to neither field. [F-AF][F-SGWL]
- When the world line shifts, Okabe's "Reading Steiner" fires. In his narration, vision goes
  **monochrome** for a moment and then colour returns. This is a natural transition effect for the
  site. [F-RS]
- The official Divergence Meter clock has an animation where the tubes spin fast and then settle
  **one tube at a time**. [FAMI-DM][OTAKU-DM]
- Tone: roughly the first half is chuunibyou comedy and otaku slice-of-life. Episode 12 is the
  hinge. The second half is a looping tragedy. [WP-TV]
- Critics describe the anime's backgrounds as muted and grayish. A reviewer noted a black-and-white
  scene in ep 11 and the recurring motif of **clasped hands**. [WP-TV]
- The 8 lab members are numbered 001–008. Their end-of-story badges read **"OSHMKUFA 2010"**
  (surname initials in joining order). [F-FGL]
- Current status on 2026-09-25: the remake **Steins;Gate RE:BOOT** came out in Japan on 2026-08-20,
  and on Steam in the West the same day. Western consoles follow on **2026-10-29**. It adds a new
  Moeka-centred "Gamma Worldline" route. [PRT-RB][SC-STEAM][SC-RB]
- 2026 is also the anime's 15th anniversary. There was an exhibition at Akihabara UDX from July 17 to
  Aug 2, 2026. [KADO-15]

## 1. Franchise facts

### 1.1 Visual novel (2009)
- **Steins;Gate** is a visual novel by **5pb.** and **Nitroplus**. It is the second main entry in the
  Science Adventure series, after Chaos;Head, and is billed as a "hypothetical science ADV"
  (想定科学ADV). [WP-SG][F-VN]
- First release: **Xbox 360, October 15, 2009** (Japan). [WP-SG]
- Later ports:
  - Windows: Aug 26, 2010
  - PSP: Jun 23, 2011
  - iOS: Aug 25, 2011
  - PS3: May 24, 2012
  - PS Vita: Mar 14, 2013
  - Android: Jun 2013
  - PS4: Dec 10, 2015 (as "Steins;Gate HD", bundled with S;G 0 first prints) [WP-SG][F-VN]
- English releases:
  - JAST USA PC: Mar 31, 2014
  - PQube PS3/Vita: 2015 (EU/AU Jun 5; US Aug 25)
  - Steam: Sep 2016
  - Spike Chunsoft took over the Steam version on May 1, 2018 [WP-SG][F-VN]
- Staff:
  - Planning and original concept: **Chiyomaru Shikura** (5pb.)
  - Scenario: **Naotaka Hayashi** (5pb.), assisted by **Vio Shimokura** (Nitroplus)
  - Character design: **huke** (Ryohei Fuke)
  - Gadget design: **Sh@rp**
  - Producer: **Tatsuya Matsubara**. Wikipedia spells it "Matsuhara"; Japanese press uses 松原達也.
  - Art director: Tosō Pehara
  - Music: **Takeshi Abo** and Toshimichi Isoe
  [WP-SG][FAMI-DM]
- Design motto: "99% science, 1% fantasy". The title comes from German "Stein" plus a nod to
  Einstein and has no specific meaning. [WP-SG]
- Stated influences: Hiroyuki Kanno's visual novels, above all **YU-NO** (1996). Shikura also named
  *Back to the Future Part II* as a direct influence. [WP-SG][WP-SA]
- Choices happen through the **"phone trigger"** system: you answer or ignore calls, and you reply to
  texts by picking underlined blue words. This is a good UI metaphor for the site. [WP-SG]
- Sales:
  - Over 1 million for the S;G games by Dec 2015 [WP-SG]
  - The official RE:BOOT site says the series has sold **over 4 million units** worldwide [RB-OFF]
- Theme songs:
  - VN opening: "Sky Clad Observer" (スカイクラッドの観測者), sung by Kanako Itō
  - True-ending theme: "Another Heaven"
  - Piano BGM "Gate of Steiner" (Abo), a strong candidate for audio [WP-SG]

### 2.1 Beat 1: Prologue (Jul 28, 2010)
- Okabe and Mayuri go to **Dr. Nakabachi's time-travel press conference at Radio Kaikan**. Okabe finds
  Kurisu Makise lying in a pool of blood and texts Daru about it. The world "blinks", and the crowds
  are suddenly gone. [WP-SG]
- A "satellite" has crashed into Radio Kaikan and the conference is cancelled. It is actually
  Suzuha's time machine (FG204), which missed its landing. [F-AKB][F-AF]
- Okabe's text arrived **a week before he sent it**. It was the first **D-Mail**. [WP-SG]
- World line: **1.130426β** before the first D-Mail (VN prologue). **0.571024α** after it.
  [F-WL]
- Episode 1 of the anime ends on the meter reading **0.571024**. [F-EP ep01]

### 2.2 Beat 2: The Lab and the PhoneWave (Jul 28 – Aug 2)
- The **Future Gadget Laboratory** sits on the 2F of a run-down building above Mr. Braun's CRT shop.
  Kurisu, alive after all, gets into a heated debate with Okabe that intrigues her enough to follow
  him to the lab. [F-FGL][F-AF]
- **PhoneWave (name subject to change)**, Future Gadget #8, is a microwave controlled by cell phone.
  It turns bananas into "**gelbanas**". [F-PW]
- Online, **John Titor** posts on @channel. The lab hacks SERN, finds the "Z Program" and the
  "jellymen" reports, and learns that SERN code can only be read by an **IBN 5100**. [F-VN][F-SERN]
- The IBN 5100 is kept at **Yanabayashi Shrine**. [F-AKB]
- This is the comedy and banter stretch: nicknames, lab-coat speeches, Round Table meetings. It is
  still 0.571024α. [F-WL]

### 2.3 Beat 3: D-Mail experiments (Operation Urd, Aug 3 – Aug 10)
The chain of shifts, in order [F-WL][F-EP]:
1. **Aug 3, Loto 6 D-Mail.** Luka misremembers one number, so no win. → **0.571015α**. The on-screen
   anime value is UNVERIFIED.
2. **Aug 4, Moeka's D-Mail** to her past self about her phone. She ends up stealing the IBN 5100 on
   Jul 31. → **0.523299α** (anime ep 8)
3. **Aug 6, Luka's D-Mail** to her mother's pager in Jan 1993 about eating vegetables. Luka is now
   born female. → **0.456903α** (anime ep 8)
4. **Aug 7, Faris's D-Mail** to her father for Apr 3, 2000. He skips the doomed flight and lives.
   Akihabara never becomes an otaku town (the otaku mecca is **Nakano** instead), and MayQueen
   becomes a ramen shop. → **0.409420α** (anime ep 9)
5. **Aug 10, Okabe's D-Mail to himself** telling him to go after Suzuha. → **0.337187α**
   (anime ep 10)

Two experiments cause no visible change: Daru's card-game tip for his match with Faris, and
Mayuri's lunch. [F-VN][F-EP]

This is the stage where Okabe finds out he alone remembers the old world line. He names this
**Reading Steiner**. [F-EP ep08]

### 2.4 Beat 4: Time Leap and the Raid (Aug 11 – Aug 13) (SPOILERS from here)
- Kurisu turns the PhoneWave into the **Time Leap Machine** (Operation Verthandi, stage 1). It sends
  your memories into your own brain up to **48 hours** in the past. [F-TLM][F-OPS]
- At the party on **Aug 13**, armed **Rounders led by Moeka** storm the lab to take the time machine,
  and **Mayuri is shot dead**. Okabe leaps back. [F-WL][F-EP ep12]
- World line: **0.337187α**. The anime episode 12 cliffhanger is the hinge of the whole series.
  [WP-TV]

### 2.5 Beat 5: Mayuri's deaths (the loop)
- Okabe time-leaps again and again. The first leap goes to **4:56 PM on Aug 13**. Mayuri dies every
  time, by gunshot, under a train, or in a crash. This is the Alpha attractor field's
  **convergence**: in every Alpha world line she dies in mid-to-late August 2010. [F-WL][F-EP ep13]
  [F-AF]
- **Suzuha reveals she is John Titor**. She comes from **2036**, a dystopia ruled by SERN. She shows
  the **Divergence Meter** (anime ep 14 reading: **0.337187**). The only escape is to cross the **1%
  divergence barrier** into the Beta field. [F-EP ep14][F-EP ep17]
- Suzuha finds her father (**Daru**) and leaves for **1975**. [F-EP ep16]
- Okabe undoes his "follow Suzuha" D-Mail. → **0.409431α** (anime ep 16). In this world line Suzuha
  died in 2000, and Mr. Braun kept the Divergence Meter and gives it to Okabe on Aug 14. [F-WL]

### 2.6 Beat 6: Undoing the D-Mails (Operation Verthandi stage 2, Aug 14 – 15)
Every D-Mail was a friend's wish. Okabe has to take each one back in reverse order to get the IBN 5100
back [F-OPS][F-EP ep17]:
- **Faris's** (her father dies again): → **0.456914α** (anime ep 17). Mayuri would still die on Aug 15
  at the ComiMa in Ariake. [F-WL]
- **Luka's** (Okabe agrees to be Luka's boyfriend for a day first): → **0.523307α** (anime ep 18).
  The IBN 5100 has already been shipped to France. **FB is revealed to be Mr. Braun**. [F-WL]
  [F-CHAR Yuugo]
- **Moeka's** (Okabe has to confront her): → **0.571046α** (anime ep 20). The IBN 5100 is back in
  the lab. [F-WL]

### 2.7 Beat 7: Paradox Meltdown, the choice (Aug 15 – 17)
- Only the **first D-Mail** is left. SERN saw it through **ECHELON**, and it is the one that saved
  Kurisu. Deleting it saves Mayuri and **sends Kurisu back to dying on Jul 28**. [F-ECH][WP-SG]
- Okabe and Kurisu confess their feelings. Daru hacks SERN with the IBN 5100, and they delete the
  record. → **1.130205β** (anime ep 22). Everyone except Okabe forgets Kurisu. [F-WL][WP-TV]
- The Mayuri ending uses the nearby value **1.130238β**. [F-WL]

### 2.8 Beat 8: Operation Skuld (Aug 21 → Jul 28)
- **Aug 21**: a Beta-line Suzuha lands a working time machine on the **Radio Kaikan rooftop**. She
  tells Okabe that Kurisu's death, and her father stealing her thesis, lead to **World War III**.
  [F-OPS][F-AKB]
- First attempt: in the scuffle with Nakabachi, **Okabe accidentally stabs Kurisu himself**. That is
  convergence again. Ep 23β branches here into S;G 0 (1.129848β). [F-OPS][F-WL]
- A garbled **video D-Mail from Okabe's future self (2025)** explains the plan. It must not change what
  past-Okabe saw (Kurisu "dead" in blood, and his text to Daru), but it has to change the outcome.
  [F-OPS]
- Second attempt:
  - Okabe **swaps Mayuri's Metal Upa for a plastic one**, so the thesis doesn't set off the metal
    detector and burns in the plane's hold.
  - He provokes Nakabachi into **stabbing him**.
  - He leaves the unconscious Kurisu lying in his blood for his past self to find.
  [F-UPA][F-SGWL][F-EP ep24]
- → **1.048596**, the **Steins Gate** world line. [F-WL][F-EP ep23]

### 2.9 Beat 9: Steins Gate (epilogue)
- On 1.048596 **neither Mayuri nor Kurisu dies**. There is neither SERN's dystopia nor WWIII. The
  thesis is destroyed, and Nakabachi is detained by Russian authorities. [F-SGWL][F-WL]
- Okabe is hospitalised with his wound (Aug 21 on this line). Weeks later, he and Kurisu meet by chance
  on an Akihabara street. She has faint memories of what happened. [F-WL][WP-SG]
- Sequel hooks: OVA ep 25 (LA trip), the 2013 film (Aug 2011, "R" line), and S;G 0 (the failed-rescue
  branch). [WP-TV][WP-MOV]

### 2.10 Values on the meter outside the main path (for easter eggs)
- **1.129848β**: S;G 0 starting line, after the failed first rescue [F-WL]
- **1.130238β**: Mayuri ending [F-WL]
- **-0.275349Ω**: Faris ending. The meter shows a blank first tube because it can't display a minus

- **0.456914α**: also the setting of the Luka ending [F-WL]
- **1.123581β**: the "Fibonacci" line, shown overlapping 1.048596 in Robotics;Notes DaSH and

- **2.615074γ**: Gamma line, Moeka-related (drama CD; now the basis of RE:BOOT's Gamma route)

## 3. Glossary (30 terms)

1. **World line**: one self-consistent history of the universe. Only one is "active" at a time.
   [F-WLP]
2. **Divergence (value)**: the number that labels a world line, measured as the difference from
   0.000000. Changes beyond the 6th decimal place don't register. [F-DV]
3. **Attractor field**: a bundle of world lines that converge on the same key events.
   **Alpha** (0.xxxxxx): Mayuri dies and SERN's dystopia follows. **Beta** (1.xxxxxx): Kurisu dies
   and WWIII follows. [F-AF]
4. **Convergence**: fate within a field. Change the details and the outcome still arrives. [F-WLP]
5. **1% barrier**: the divergence you need to cross to jump from the Alpha to the Beta field.
   [F-EP ep17]
6. **Steins Gate world line**: 1.048596, between the two fields and ruled by neither. Okabe named it.
   [F-SGWL]
7. **Reading Steiner**: Okabe's ability to keep his memories across a world-line shift. It feels like
   vertigo and his vision drains to monochrome. [F-RS]
8. **D-Mail**: short for "DeLorean Mail", a text sent into the past through the PhoneWave. The limit
   is 36 bytes. [F-DMAIL][F-RWR]
9. **PhoneWave (name subject to change)**: 電話レンジ（仮）, Future Gadget #8. It is a phone-controlled
   microwave that makes micro black holes. [F-PW]
10. **Gelbana**: a banana turned to green gel by the PhoneWave. It was the first clue. [F-PW]
11. **42" CRT**: Mr. Braun's giant Braun-tube TV directly below the lab. Its cathode rays turn the
    black holes into stable Kerr black holes. [F-PW][F-GL]
12. **Time Leap Machine**: Kurisu's upgrade. It compresses your memories and sends them into your own
    brain at most **48 hours** back, received by phone. [F-TLM]
13. **Divergence Meter**: 8 Nixie tubes built by future Okabe, showing the current divergence. See
    section 6. [F-DM]
14. **Nixie tube**: a neon-glow numeric display tube from the 1950s. The digits 0–9 are stacked
    cathodes lit by roughly 170 V. [FAMI-DM]
15. **El Psy Kongroo**: Okabe's sign-off on fake "Organization" phone calls. The official VN spelling
    is "Kongroo", while anime subs often use "Congroo". [F-GL] LOW
16. **Hououin Kyouma** (鳳凰院 凶真): Okabe's mad-scientist alter ego and "true name". [WP-SG]
17. **The Organization**: the imaginary agency Okabe claims is hunting him. The irony is that it
    becomes real as SERN. [F-GL] LOW
18. **"The choice of Steins Gate"**: Okabe's catchphrase for anything that happens. He admits it
    means nothing. [F-GL][F-CHAR Okabe]
19. **Lab Mem (Lab Member) 001–011**: Okabe's numbered inductees. 001–008 appear in S;G, and 009–011
    were added in S;G 0. [F-FGL]
20. **OSHMKUFA 2010**: the lab-mem badge. The letters are surname initials in joining order, and
    2010 is the lab's founding year. [F-FGL]

## 6. The Divergence Meter

### 6.1 In-story origin
- It was **invented by the future Okabe for his own use**. After he died, it passed to **Daru**, who
  gave it to his daughter **Suzuha**. She brought it back from 2036 to Okabe in 2010. It is a closed
  loop. [F-DM]
- On world line 0.409431, Suzuha dies in 2000 and **Mr. Braun keeps the meter**. He hands it to Okabe
  on Aug 14, 2010. [F-WL]
- Anime: Suzuha first shows it at Radio Kaikan (ep 14), reading 0.337187. [F-EP ep14]
- How it works in the fiction:
  1. It records a baseline reading of local gravity.
  2. After time travel, it compares that baseline against the new gravity reading.
  3. It shows the difference as a % from world line 0.000000, the line where the first meter was
     built.
  [F-DM][F-DV]
- Producer Matsubara: in the setting, the meter is built from **parts you can buy in Akihabara parts
  shops**. [DENGEKI-DM][OTAKU-DM]
- Only Okabe can "read" a change, because everyone else's memories are rewritten along with the
  number. [F-DM]

### 6.2 Display format
- It uses **8 Nixie tubes**. [TARI][BROTORO]
- It shows a **7-digit** divergence value (世界線変動率を7桁の数字で表す). [DENGEKI-DM]
- Pattern: `D.DDDDDD`, with 1 integer digit and 6 decimals. For example, `1.048596` or `0.571024`.
- The decimal point appears to take **its own tube**: 8 tubes for 7 digits, and one replica builder
  notes that it only needs a dot lit in a tube with no digit. Read it as tube 1 = integer digit, tube
  2 = decimal point only, tubes 3–8 = decimals. This is **inferred** (UNVERIFIED against a frame
  grab), so check a screenshot before building. [BROTORO]
- **Negative (Omega) values**: the first tube is left **blank**, because the meter can't show a minus
  sign. [F-DM]
- The meter can show **two overlapping values** at once, for example 1.048596 and 1.123581 in
  Robotics;Notes DaSH and Anonymous;Code. [F-DM]
- Writing convention: fans write "1.048596%". The wiki swaps the % for the field's Greek letter
  (`0.571024α`, `1.130205β`). [F-WLP]
- Shift animation:
  - The VN animates the digits scrambling before they settle. [BROTORO]
  - The official clock spins the tubes fast and **stops them one at a time**. [FAMI-DM]

### 6.3 Nixie tube models
- Prop accuracy:
  - **No real tube matches the prop exactly**. Its tubes are smooth-topped, about 15 mm across, and
    need a decimal point. [BROTORO]
  - Fans often call the look **IN-18**, but the IN-18 has **no decimal points** and is about 30 mm
    wide. [BROTORO][TARI]
- **IN-14** (Soviet) is the **most common replica choice**. It is cheap, side-view, and has left and
  right decimal points. [BROTORO][HACKADAY][GH-4BF][TARI]
- Other tubes used:
  - Burroughs B5441A and National 5441 (smooth top, rare) [BROTORO]
  - IN-8-2 and NL-5440A (Nixie Clocks shop options) [NCS]
- **IN-12**: no documented divergence-meter replica using it was found. It is a top-view tube, so it
  doesn't fit the side-view prop. UNVERIFIED.

## 7. Tone and themes to convey

- **Comedy → tragedy.** The first half is chuunibyou theatre, otaku in-jokes and lab banter, and it
  deliberately lulls you. ANN's Carlo Santos praised how it "misleads" you with comedy before turning
  into a thriller. Episode 12 is the hook, and the finale loops back to episode 1's scene. [WP-TV]
  - Design idea: a warm, cluttered, jokey top half that goes quiet and grey partway down the scroll.
- **The cost of changing the past.**
  - Every D-Mail granted someone's wish: Moeka's phone, Luka's body, Faris's father, Suzuha's
    search.
  - Saving Mayuri means **taking each wish back personally**, face to face.
  - Kurisu's view is that she wouldn't erase even her failures.
  [F-EP ep17][F-CHAR Kurisu]
- **Convergence and fate.** Inside an attractor field, the outcome is fixed. The only way out is a
  large enough change, the "1% barrier", or the narrow gap between the fields. [F-AF][F-EP ep17]
- **"The choice of Steins Gate."** It starts as a meaningless catchphrase. By the end it names a real
  choice: a world chosen, not granted. [F-GL][F-SGWL]
- **Deceive the world.** The resolution does not change what was observed (Kurisu "dead" in blood). It
  changes what happened. [F-OPS]
- **Loneliness of the observer, and trauma.** Okabe alone remembers every world line. Critics
  singled out the show's portrayal of **PTSD**, and S;G 0 makes it explicit. [WP-TV][WP-0]
- **The persona as love.** Hououin Kyouma began as a performance to keep Mayuri from slipping away.
  In the film, the persona itself becomes what saves him. [F-CHAR Okabe][WP-MOV]
- **Real science as texture.**
  - The "99% science" feel comes from real names and ideas: John Titor, the IBM 5100, CERN and the
    LHC, ECHELON, Kerr black holes, Nixie tubes.
  - Shikura set the series in reality so it would feel believable.
  [WP-SA][F-RWR]
- **Mood references:**
  - Abo described Steins;Gate's sound as "cloudy", compared with Chaos;Head "rainy" and
    Robotics;Notes "clear". [WP-SA]
  - The anime's Akihabara was praised as "lifeless but ever moving", with muted grey backgrounds.
    [WP-TV]
  - Recurring visual motifs: clasped hands, the monochrome Reading Steiner flash, the glow of Nixie
    tubes.
